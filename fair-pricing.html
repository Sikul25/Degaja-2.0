Transparency, Not a Subscription Trap

At DEGAJA, you see the price of a reading before you pay. There's no subscription, no automatic renewal, and the consent checkbox before checkout is never pre-checked. That's not a marketing line — it's how our payment system is actually built.

Why this matters right now

In June 2026, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) filed a lawsuit against a network of companies called "Genesis Tech" (Case No. 4:26-cv-05232-HSG, U.S. District Court, Northern District of California) — which, according to the complaint, includes the app Nebula. The FTC alleges that wording like "Personalized reading for $5" created the impression of a one-time purchase, while a $45-every-30-days subscription was buried in the fine print. A federal court has temporarily halted the business's operations.

These allegations have not been decided by any court — this is an active, pending case, not a final ruling.

Source: FTC press release, Case No. 4:26-cv-05232-HSG.
